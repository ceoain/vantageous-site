Privacy policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

Vantageous is a business name of Ain Corp., an Ontario corporation (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what personal information we collect through vantageous.studio and in our work with clients, why, and what choices you have. We follow Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect

  • When you contact us or fill in the project form: your name, work email, company, website, what you need, your launch timing and budget range, and anything you write in your message.
  • When you book a call: the name, email and time you give the booking tool.
  • When you visit the site: anonymous, aggregated visit statistics (pages viewed, referrer, country, device type) through Vercel Web Analytics, which does not use cookies or identify you.
  • When you become a client: the contact, billing and project information needed to do the work and invoice you.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, prepare for and hold the call you book, and send proposals you ask for.
  • To deliver projects, manage invoices and keep business and tax records.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the site is used so we can improve it.

We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

Who processes it for us

We use a small number of service providers to run the business: Vercel (website hosting and analytics), our form provider (to receive form submissions), Google Workspace (email and calendar booking), and our accounting and e-signature tools. They process information only on our behalf. Some are based in the United States, so your information may be stored or processed outside Canada and be subject to the laws of those countries.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for up to 2 years if they don’t become a project, and client records for as long as tax and legal rules require (generally 6 years in Canada).

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw consent to us using it, by emailing hello@vantageous.studio. We’ll respond within 30 days. If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Security

We protect personal information with reasonable safeguards, including access controls and two-factor authentication on the accounts that hold it.
