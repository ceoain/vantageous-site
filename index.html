Work

Our own launch film, made the way we'd make yours.

“The marble run” tells our story the way a launch film should tell yours: a brand in motion breaks out of its box and takes off. 45 seconds, delivered with 15s and 30s cuts and in 9:16, 4:5 and 16:9.

  1. 0:00

    The hook

    A marble bounces along a steady line inside a glass box. The question lands from the first frame.

  2. 0:08

    The split

    It hits our logo and peels onto an exponential path while the steady line carries on unlit.

  3. 0:12

    The breakout

    The corner of the box cracks and shatters on the beat. The open box with the marble outside is our logo.

  4. 0:16

    The trajectory

    The scene turns into a growth chart that draws itself on: steady line versus the exponential one.

  5. 0:26

    New markets

    Branches grow beyond the box into six animations, each taking centre stage, then fold back into the logo.

3D scenes, type and music built in code; photoreal shots made with AI video. Original score, owned outright.

Process

From brief to launch in four steps.

  1. 1

    Brief

    A 30-minute call about your product, audience and launch. We send a one-page brief to sign off.

  2. 2

    Concept and keyframes

    The idea, the script and stills of the key shots. You approve them before any video is made.

  3. 3

    Make

    3D, AI footage, motion type and an original score, cut to the beat. Two rounds of revisions.

  4. 4

    Launch and measure

    Every format, ready to post or run as ads. On a retainer, we test hooks and report what works.

FAQ

Questions founders ask us.

Do you use AI?

Yes, openly. AI video and image tools help us make studio-grade work in weeks instead of months. The story, design, 3D, edit and music are directed by a person, and we only use tools whose terms allow commercial use.

Who owns the work?

You do. Once the project is paid, you own the final films and assets and can use them anywhere. We keep our tools and project files, and we license you the original score for use with your films.

How long does it take?

A Launch Reel takes about 3 weeks from an approved brief, and a Launch Campaign 4–5 weeks. Rush delivery is available.

Do you work with fintech and crypto companies?

Yes, it's a specialty. We plan claims, risk disclosures and each platform's financial-services ad policy from the first draft, and you approve every claim in writing before anything runs.

Will you run our ads?

On a Growth Retainer, yes. We work inside your own ad accounts through partner access, so you keep ownership of your accounts and data. Ad spend is paid by you directly to the platforms.

Where are you based?

Ontario, Canada. We work remotely with startups across Canada and the US.
