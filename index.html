Why fintech

Creative that clears review, not just the feed.

Financial ads live under stricter rules than almost anything else on social. We plan for them from the first draft, so your launch doesn't stall on a rejected ad.

  • Risk lines built in. On screen and in the caption, sized to be read, not hidden.
  • No promised returns. We show the product working, never a profit figure it can't back up.
  • Platform policy first. Every concept is checked against the ad rules of the platforms and markets you target.
  • Your compliance team signs off. We build their review into the schedule, not around it.
